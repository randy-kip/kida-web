IDEAS WE'D LOVE TO PROPOSE

A Government Workflow Platform

Six configurable products for public-sector service delivery — each close to the automation we already build, each demonstrable with one narrow use case before it scales across agencies.

1

Service Request & Case Management

Citizens, businesses, or staff submit requests via web, WhatsApp, SMS, USSD, or call centre. The platform classifies, routes to the right department, tracks deadlines, sends updates, and escalates delays — for complaints, permits, inspections, licensing and more.

2

Forms, Applications & Approvals

Agencies create digital forms without new software each time, define approval steps, collect and verify documents, assign officers, and issue approvals, certificates, or renewals — for permits, licences, grants, scholarships, tenders and registrations.

3

Inter-Agency Data Exchange

Connects existing systems through secure APIs, standardises data exchange, manages permissions, and shows which agency owns the next step — so citizens stop re-submitting the same details. Interoperability first, not replacement. (Rwanda's IremboGov: 100+ services, 20+ agencies.)

4

Grants, Programs & Benefits

Manages the full lifecycle of a public program: applications, eligibility checks, beneficiary records, approvals, disbursements, field verification, reporting, and audits — for youth funds, bursaries, agricultural programs, disaster relief and NGO funding.

5

Inspections & Field Operations

Inspectors receive assignments, use mobile forms, capture photos and GPS, record findings, issue notices, schedule follow-ups, and track corrective action — for health, building, environmental, labour, transport and food-safety enforcement.

6

Citizen Engagement Layer

Unifies WhatsApp, SMS, email, voice, web chat, and USSD so agencies can send reminders, answer common questions, and update people on applications. AI handles triage and FAQs — always on top of clear workflows, permissions, and human escalation.

★

The strongest first product: a configurable Government Workflow OS

Start with Service Request & Case Management or Forms & Approvals — broad enough to work across sectors, closest to the automation we already deliver, and easy to prove with one narrow use case before expanding. Package the same core for county services, healthcare regulation, agriculture, licensing, or procurement without rebuilding it each time.
